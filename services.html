Process

How a project goes from an idea to a link you can share.

  1. 01

    Idea

    We talk about what you need, who it is for and the content you already have.

  2. 02

    Design

    I plan the pages and the look, and you approve the direction before I code.

  3. 03

    Development

    I build it by hand, test it on phones and computers and adjust it with your feedback.

  4. 04

    Launch

    I publish it online and send you the link, ready to share.
