The short version

I'm Jair M. Morales Magallon, a Cybersecurity student at Universidad Tecnológica de Panamá. Since July 2026 I also teach programming at Centro ¡Supérate! Fundación Alberto Motta in La Chorrera, and I build websites for myself and for other people.

Most of what I build starts with a person or an occasion: a quinceañera that needed an invitation, a graduation gift, a Christmas greeting, a community project that needed a home online. I make pages in any style, from presentation letters and invitations to informational and fully functional sites.

How I got here

I started programming in the technology courses at Centro ¡Supérate!, and what began as a class quickly turned into the thing I wanted to do every day. Today I'm back there on the other side of the room, teaching programming, web development and digital skills to younger students.

I chose cybersecurity after discovering it in class. It's one of the fastest-growing fields in tech, and the more I learned, the more I realized that building something and protecting it are two halves of the same job. I'm now studying it at the Universidad Tecnológica de Panamá.

What motivates me

My goal is secure web development: building sites and apps that look good and are hard to break. Outside of class, I'm always improving my projects, customizing things to work exactly how I want, and exploring how AI works and how to get the most out of it.

I want to build software that feels finished.

I don't add technology to a project just because it is popular. Every tool should have a purpose, and the result should be clean code, thoughtful design and useful functionality.
